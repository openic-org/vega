## Decisions (Manuel)

- **`CHK_CLEAR` reads back as 0.** Every write stores the whole word in
  `ram[addr_reg]`, and reads come from the RAM, not the mirror registers.
  So after a clear, word 232 read back with bit 4 set, as did the
  `REG_WRITE16` ack, and a read-modify-write of 232 (e.g. changing only
  `mosi_sel`) would clear the checker again. Manuel masks bit 4 in the
  RAM write for word 232. Spec line (`spi1-mosi-eye-scan.md` §, word 232)
  to update once that is in.
- **R3 deferred until after the animal test, and widened.** Named defines
  for 232/233 only would be inconsistent with the other live words, which
  are all `RB_CTRL_BASE + 8'dN`. Afterwards: one define for every used
  word. Moved to PLAN.md B.3.
