First run of the SCLK-clocked RHD2164 emulator against the real Intan
controller. **RHX's port scan found two RHD2164s, 128 channels, on the
first attempt.** No Kuntur, no tunnel: the companion answered from a ROM.

## What the emulator is

`kuntur` `kuntur144/fpga/intan_rec_adapter/`, rewritten 2026-10-13 by
Manuel (Claude reviewed). Spec: `docs/interfaces/lvds-tunnel.md` §9.1a,
§9.1b.

- **Clocked by the controller's SCLK** (`spi0_sck`, `PCLKT3_2`, a primary
  clock input); CSb asynchronously resets the bit counter. No
  oversampling. The 12 MHz oscillator drives only the LED.
- **ROM only.** 64 × 8 `RHD_REG*` values from `rhd2164_defs.vh`, inferred
  into one EBR (`SP16K`); contents checked in the netlist `INITVAL`s.
  Updated only on `READ`; any other command's answer repeats the last
  `READ` value.
- **Two-word pipeline:** `data_rx` latches word N−1 at its 16th falling
  edge → ROM read at word N's 1st rising edge → `data_tx` at the 2nd
  rising edge → TX register at the 16th rising edge → shifted out in word
  N+1.
- MISO A during SCLK high, MISO B during SCLK low; all four streams carry
  the same data, so synthesis merged them into one register.

An earlier draft of the same day loaded the TX register asynchronously on
CSb falling, in a block with `rstb`, `sck` and `csb` all tested in the
`if` chain. That leaves no clock for synthesis to infer; it simulated
correctly but would not have built as intended. Fixed before this run.
The first synthesis of the rewrite also ran with the wrong top-level
module (`…_oversampling`, Radiant's own pick); corrected.

## What this settles

- **MOSI polarity.** An inverted `READ` (`11rrrrrr 00000000`) decodes as a
  `CONVERT`; the ROM would never have answered.
- **MISO1 and MISO2 polarity.** An inverted line returns the complement of
  `"INTAN"` and fails `getChipID()`. Both lines passed.
  With CS and SCLK (2026-10-10) and VDD orientation, **every signal on the
  C3430 harness is now verified**. The MOSI LED-check bitstream proposed
  on 10-10 is no longer needed.
- **Pipeline depth is exactly two words.** One word off shifts every
  answer one sample index and fails at all 16 delays (§9.1b).
- **SCLK clocking works at the controller's real SCLK.** §9.1a's design
  rule was argued from simulation; it now holds on hardware.

## What it does not settle

- **Delay margin.** RHX does not print the chosen MISO delay or how many
  of the 16 settings passed. Read the per-port cable delay from the
  Configure tab next session and record it as the baseline. A margin of
  one setting would pass today and fail on a different cable.
- **SCLK frequency.** Still the inferred 21 MHz (2800 × fs / 4), not
  measured.
- **Any waveform.** `CONVERT` answers are stale `READ` values, so the
  traces are meaningless. Nothing about data, the §9.5 frame switch, or
  `CONVERT(0)` as the sweep start is tested.
- **The testbench** still sends `READ(63)` on every word and could not
  have caught a wrong pipeline depth. RHX caught nothing because there was
  nothing to catch; the testbench should still be fixed before the next
  RTL change (§9.1b, consequence 1). *Fixed later the same day, see
  below.*

## Testbench: RHX command sequence

`tb.v` rewritten to replay what RHX actually sends (§9.1b), committed in
`kuntur` as `ba57867` (merge `c330952`); `qrun.f` changed `run 1us` →
`run -all` as `5eaa2f4` (merge `362e57c`), because `run 1us` stopped
after about one word.

- **Stimulus.** Behavioural SPI master at 21 MHz SCLK, 20 periods per
  word (16 data + CS high), MOSI changed after falling edges; MISO A
  sampled T/4 after each rising edge, MISO B T/4 after each falling edge.
  40 sample periods × 35 words (~1.33 ms): `CONVERT(0..31)`, then
  AuxCmd1 (`WRITE(3)`), AuxCmd2 (`CONVERT(32/33/34)`, `READ(40)`,
  `CONVERT(48)`), AuxCmd3 (the 60-command bank-0 register-config list
  with `CALIBRATE`), built as RHX builds them.
- **Check 1.** Every `READ` answered exactly two words later as
  `{8'h00, value}` on MISO A and B of both lines; a mismatch reports
  whether the answer matches depth 1 or 3 instead. Non-`READ` answers are
  counted, not checked (ROM-only: they repeat the last `READ`).
- **Check 2.** RHX `getChipID()` repeated on the AuxCmd3 results — index
  19 chip ID, 23 register 59, 24–26 "RHD", 32–36 "INTAN" — against
  literal values, not the `rhd2164_defs.vh` macros.

**Result (iverilog, `-g2012 -DSIM`, against the RTL in `54084a5`):**
`TB RESULT: PASS`, 32 `READ`s checked, detection passes on MISO1 and
MISO2. Register 59 on MISO B reads 53 where the spec wants 58; reported
as KNOWN OPEN, not a failure.

**Mutation check.** A scratch copy of the RTL with one extra word of
delay on line-0 MISO A: `FAIL`, 25 errors, MISO1 detection fails and
MISO2 passes, depth-3 diagnostic fires. The testbench now catches the
pipeline-depth error the old one could not.

**Questa:** Manuel ran the new `tb.v` in Questa (`qrun.f`, `run -all`)
and it passed, so the result holds on both simulators.

**Still open:** `wave.do` may still name the old testbench's `spi1_*`
signals. The slot order — `CONVERT` first, aux commands at 32–34 — is still inferred
from RHX source, not observed on the wire.

## Context — spec decisions, 2026-10-12/13

Merged as `3ec64ec` (`fc53b21`):

- **`TUN_CLK` forwards `ECLK` (42.5 MHz), not the ÷4 word clock.** The
  word clock (10.6 MHz) is below the LIFCL PLL's 18 MHz reference
  minimum, so the companion PLL in §6.6 could never have locked. Byte
  alignment is now found by preamble hunt + `IDDRX4.ALIGNWD` (new O2b).
- **Gearbox word clock renamed `WCLK`**, so "SCLK" means only the SPI
  clock.
- **§9.1b** documents what RHX sends, from RHX source `3c58677`.
- **§9.3's absorbed-write counter** would have counted ~30,000/s (RHX
  writes register 3 every sample period). Replaced by
  `host_write_changes` and the `host_mismatch_regs` bitmap.
