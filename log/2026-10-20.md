## K1 + K2: ports, LVDS, edge-clock chain (RTL by Manuel)

`kuntur` `kuntur144/fpga/kuntur_fpga/`, on top of `bdbe1fd`, **not
committed**:

- Ports renamed `serial_lvds_tx` → `tun_data` (G9/F9) and
  `serial_lvds_rx` → `tun_clk` (E9/E8). The `TEST ONLY` MISO passthrough
  assigns are removed; `cmd_is_00 = fifo_full` stays.
- `.pdc`: `IO_TYPE=LVDS` on both; the stale `serial_lvds_*` lines are
  deleted. `mregion0`–`mregion7` untouched.
- Four primitives, the topology of Lattice's own ECLK-aligned TX IP
  (`ip/lifcl/ddr/rtl/lscc_gddrx2_4_5_tx_eclk_aligned_static_bypass.v`):

```
clk ─> ECLKSYNC (STOP_EN="ENABLE", STOP=0) ─ eclk ─┬─> ECLKDIV (÷4, GSR="DISABLED") ─ wclk
                                                   ├─> ODDRX4  D = 1,1,0,0,1,1,0,0  ─> tun_data
                                                   └─> ODDRX4  D = 1,0,1,0,1,0,1,0  ─> tun_clk
tun_rst = ~rstb  ─> ECLKDIV.DIVRST, both ODDRX4.RST
```

`GSR="DISABLED"` on `ECLKDIV` copies Lattice: its ÷4 phase should be set
only by `DIVRST`, together with the gearbox resets. The
STOP → reset → release start-up sequence (`gddr_sync.v` in Lattice's IP)
is **not** implemented. Constant patterns cannot show a gearbox phase
error, so it is needed before real frames, not for this test.

**`` `default_nettype none `` is commented out** in `kuntur_fpga.v`. It
breaks Radiant's generated `pll0.v`, probably because the directive
carries over into files compiled after it. Likely fix: a trailing
`` `default_nettype wire ``. Deferred by Manuel. The first build without
it had `tun_rst` undeclared and undriven (synthesis: "Patching with
GND"); fixed before the bitstream below.

**O2a closed:** PAR routed `clk` (`pll0` `CLKOP`) through `ECLKSYNC` into
bank 5's edge clock. `ECLKSYNC` is required, since it is what puts a
clock on the network the gearboxes use.

**Bit order:** the Radiant `ODDRX4` model says *"D0 is sent out first and
D7 last"*. With byte bit 0 on `D0` the link is LSB-first, and the
companion's `IDDRX4` mirrors it.

**SPI1 margins (O3) not settled.** Tightest path `spi1_csb` +0.761 ns
setup; MISO capture +1.96 to +2.18 ns; MOSI +10.80 ns. There is no
baseline for `HEAD` without the tunnel: Radiant reports are untracked, and
the recorded CSB figures (+0.417 ns on 08-31, +1.46 ns in
`fpga-timing-constraints.md` §9.2) are from earlier designs. A `HEAD`
build in a scratch worktree would give one; not done.

## Bench: both pairs toggle (Manuel)

The 17:28 bitstream is loaded. On the scope, **`tun_clk` ≈ 45 MHz and
`tun_data` ≈ 22.7 MHz**, against the expected 45.54 and 22.77 MHz.

- **One leg of each pair probed, no 100 Ω termination.** Frequency is
  valid. Levels and edge shape are not, since an unterminated LVDS driver
  has no defined swing, and the DSOX1204G's bandwidth is marginal for
  45 MHz anyway.
- **pc-app not run.** Whether SPI1 and both chips are unaffected with the
  tunnel toggling (O3 on hardware) is untested.
