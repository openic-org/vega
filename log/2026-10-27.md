# 2026-10-27 — Receiver state flow reviewed; `cdr_tb` reworked for the whole `cdr`

Continues `log/2026-10-26.md`. Error counting (ALIGN against CHECK errors,
the bit-error count) is deferred by Manuel; nothing here covers it.

## `cdr_fsm` / `frame_aligner` / `frame_checker` review (Manuel's RTL)

Three rounds, each simulated on a scratch copy of the RTL with three
streams: clean (at bit offsets 0–7), no preamble (ALIGN fails), and
preamble with a `02 80` tail (CHECK fails).

- **Simulation hang** (zero-delay loop between the `cdr_fsm` and
  `frame_checker` comb blocks): caused by the old `op2d → op2a` path,
  which raised `checker_start` while the checker sat in its finished
  state. Gone once every check goes through `op1a` (checker reset). The
  RTL was correct as hardware; Manuel's call.
- **After a step, back to `idle`**: resets the aligner and checker, and
  waits for `lock`.
- **`bitsel` moved into `frame_aligner`** (Manuel's proposal, agreed).
  First version advanced `bitsel` every cycle of the search, including
  the cycle of the match: a clean stream never locked. Fixed: advance
  only when a 15-byte window ends with no match.
- **`cnt0` left mid-window at a restart.** Not a power-up issue (the
  FPGA starts every register at 0; Manuel's point, accepted). It was
  about restarts: with `cnt0` not reset by `rst_sync`, the first window at
  `bitsel` 0 could be 1–15 bytes long, and 18 of 30 restarts on a clean
  offset-0 stream reported ALIGN fail. Fixed: the aligner's `idle` pulses
  `rst_sync_2`. 0 of 30 after.
- **Phase 279 never tested**: fixed, `op2c` checks the maximum before
  stepping. All 280 phases are tried; the last pulse returns the PLL to
  0°.
- **`op3` and `op4` both set `rdy`**: fixed, `op3` also sets `valid`.
- **Settle time after PHASESTEP** (raised by Claude, withdrawn):
  TN-02095 rev 2.5 §14.5.9/§14.6.1: the phase changes 4 VCO cycles
  (about 2.5 ns at 1594 MHz) after the falling edge; PHASESTEP must stay
  low and then high for 4 VCO cycles each; delaying is glitch-free.
  Nothing says `LOCK` drops. One `wclk` in `op2d` is enough.

## `cdr_tb.sv` reworked

The separate checker instance (`WITH_CHECKER`, `link_checker` stub) is
gone: the checker is now inside `cdr`. Three parts:

1. **Sweep** (tb sets the phase), as before, plus:
   - `frame_aligner` alone: valid, with `bitsel` = `rx_ref`'s offset, at
     every error-free step; `dout`/`pos` check unchanged;
   - `cdr` reset at every step and run to its first decision there (lock,
     ALIGN fail or CHECK fail). It must lock with the right `bitsel` at
     every eye-interior step, and must not lock where `rx_ref` has no
     lock.
2. **Closed loop**: PHASESTEP drives the PLL model (one step per pulse,
   sampled on the falling edge of `wclk`). Start in the middle of the
   widest no-lock region; `cdr` must end locked at a step where `rx_ref`
   locked, and its phase counter must equal the pulse count.
3. **No data** (`din` = 0): `cdr` must give up (`rdy`, not `valid`) after
   exactly 280 pulses, back at the start phase.

Findings from the tb itself:

- **The word boundary moves 2 bits per full turn.** A 360° turn of
  `CLKOS` delays the clock by one period while ECLKDIV keeps counting
  edges. So the right `bitsel` at a given phase drops by 2 per turn.
  The real PLL does the same. The tb counts turns and corrects for it.
- **First lock lands at the edge of the eye.** From the middle of a
  closed region the closed loop locks at the first step whose checker
  sees two clean blocks: step 111 in the default run, where `rx_ref`
  counted 60 bit errors in 100 blocks. A first-lock search is not an
  operating point; that needs the §6.6 sweep and centring (open
  decision, Manuel).
- **PHASESTEP glitches in simulation.** In some runs PHASESTEP had twice
  as many falling edges as pulses: zero-width glitches, because
  `phasestep` is a comb FSM output, assigned 1 by default and then 0. The
  tb filters them. On hardware PHASESTEP is edge-triggered by the PLL, so
  it should come from a register (it also has to move to `clkin`, §6.4).
  For Manuel.

Not covered: `lock` low (tied to 1), lock-loss return to `idle`, and the
