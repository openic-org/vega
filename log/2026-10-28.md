## Error count in `frame_checker`

Requirements listed from §6.6 first (seed the expected `cnt` once, +1 at
each `pos` 7 → 0; all 64 bits; no re-hunt; saturate at `FFFF`; N =
`NB_ERRORS`). New unit test `tb/frame_checker_tb.v` (kuntur): an ideal
aligned stream, reset at each of the 8 byte positions; modes clean,
`+inj=k`, `+flood` (every bit wrong, 64 × N), `+dead` (no lock).

Three rounds:

- `frame_b4`/`frame_b5` undriven (`errors` = X); `errors_en` a latch (no
  default); window ended at `NB_CHECK` (100) instead of `NB_ERRORS`, and
  `frames` wrapping at `NB_ERRORS` meant that with N = 20 < 100 neither
  `done` nor the no-lock timeout ever came; expected `cnt` stepped by a
  free-running counter, not `pos`, and seeded without + 1; saturation
  could wrap (`FFFE` + 8).
- Then: still the `frames` wrap (no-lock never fired at N = 20, `+dead`
  hung); window 8N + 1 bytes (flood gave 1288 for 1280; `+inj` cannot
  see it, the extra byte is a fixed one); output could show the wrapped
  sum for one cycle when crossing `FFFF` on the last byte. Typo
  `frame_is_` / `frames_is_` survived only as an implicit wire.
- Then all six runs PASS (N = 20: clean, `+inj=4`, flood, dead;
  N = 1100 and N = 1024 flood, the latter crossing `FFFF` on the window's
  last byte).

The count window starts at `pos` 1 of the block after the two clean
blocks, the same point every time.

## Sweep controller in `cdr_fsm` / `cdr`

Requirements listed from §6.6 and from what `cdr_tb` checks. Rounds:

1. **Clean if `errors` = 0 alone** made ALIGN failures and checker
   no-locks clean (both leave `errors` at 0): with no data it would have
   reported success. Fixed: clean = aligned, locked, 0 errors.
   `best_len` was a 1-bit port.
2. **`meas_en` one cycle ahead of `meas_*`** at the ports (internal
   capture was right; Manuel asked what "export" meant): the port is now
   a registered copy. **Trackers not cleared in `idle`**: Manuel asked
   whether that is not what `rstb` is for. Answer: `rstb` is the global
   reset (power-up, start-up sequence), but a re-lock failure and a lock
   loss both start a new sweep through `idle` with `rstb` high, so
   per-sweep state belongs to `idle`.
3. **Run tracker**, two rounds. Off-by-one starts, the first run never a
   candidate, the last run never compared (no `meas_en` after 279), the
   join missing step 279, all-clean giving `FAIL` (later 558 wrapping to
   46), the join applied when 279 was bad, and a + 1 bias that let a
   longer run lose. Replaced by a running maximum on clean steps (spec
   §6.6 *As built*). Tested on its own against `cdr_tb`'s rule: 3,000
   sweeps, all match.
4. **Go to the centre.** First version merged the 279 step with the
   first centre pulse, then gave 1-`wclk` pulses and pulsed once more at
   the centre (the count came out right by luck). Restructured:
   `op4a` → `WAIT` (8 high) → `MOVE_TO_CENTER` (check first) →
   `MTC_LOW` (2 low). `phase` and `eye_center` were 1-bit inputs to
   `cdr_fsm`. `rdy`/`valid`/`eye_width` were undriven ports. Port
   renamed `centre` → `eye_center` in the tb and spec (Manuel).
5. **Re-lock.** The first `cdr_tb` run ended at the centre after
   280 + 176 pulses with `bitsel` 7 for 6: the 2-bit shift a full turn
   causes (Manuel asked for the reason again; explained: a turn delays
   ECLK one period = 2 bits while ECLKDIV keeps counting). Re-lock added;
   first without `checker_start` (checker idle, no `done`), then
   `RELOCK_3` looping to `RELOCK_1` (checker reset every 3 cycles). Both
   fixed.
6. **Lock loss**: `lock` in every state through a two-flop synchroniser,
   first clocked by `clk` (12 MHz) while the FSM runs on `wclk`, a
   crossing the simulator cannot see. Moved to `wclk`.

## `cdr_tb` change: `meas_phase` check race

The defaults run (1 step) and `+q7first` (8 steps) reported
`meas_phase` off the PLL. Not the RTL: on the no-lock paths the FSM
starts the step's `PHASESTEP` pulse in the same cycle as its
measurement pulse; the port `meas_en` is one `wclk` later, and when the
12 MHz edge falls just after the register changes, the pin falls about
86 ns later, before the next `wclk` edge (87.8 ns). The tb now compares
against the PLL step as of the previous `wclk` edge (`step_prev`).

## Result

`cdr_tb` **ALL PASS** on `+n=20`, `+n=20 +inj=4`, `+n=20 +start=150`,
`+n=20 +seed=7 +jitter=1000` and the defaults. `+q7first` reports 9
FAIL, as designed: nothing can lock with the bit order reversed, and
`cdr` gives up after one turn (`rdy`, not `valid`) both times.
Defaults: centre step 176, `eye_width` 128 steps, 280 + 176 pulses,
`bitsel` 6 at the centre; after the lock drop, one new sweep and the
same centre. `frame_checker_tb`: PASS.

Committed: `kuntur` `46d47a3` (branch `session-2026-10-28-rx-sweep`).

## T2 start-up sequence: requirements and decisions

Sources: §6.4, PLAN K3.4, TN-02097 §9.1.1 (ports only, no timing), and
Lattice's `gddr_sync.v` (Radiant 2025.2.1, `ip/lifcl/ddr/rtl/`), whose
state machine gives the timing: wait 3 cycles of `START`, `STOP` 4,
`STOP` + `DDR_RESET` 4, `STOP` 4, release, `READY`.

Found while listing them:

- Both ends tie `ECLKSYNC.STOP` to 0 and drive `DIVRST` and the gearbox
  `RST` straight from `~rstb`.
- `gddr_sync` runs once per reset (`stop_assert` saturates), so its
  `RST` must include the lock: `!rstb || !lock_sync`.
- Kuntur's start-up clock must be its `clkin` (32 MHz), not `clk`: `clk`
  feeds `ECLKSYNC`, and the TN rules out the interface clock. K3.4 said
  "on `clk`"; corrected.
- Kuntur's `pll0` was generated with `LOCK_EN(0)` and no `lock_o`.

Decisions (Manuel):

- (a) instantiate `gddr_sync` on both ends rather than write one;
- (b) retrying `cdr` from `fail` (clock present, no data) goes to §7;
- (c) regenerate Kuntur's `pll0` now with the lock output (same
  45.54 MHz) rather than a fixed delay after `rstb`; R1 keeps it.

Simulated `gddr_sync` with this wiring (scratch tb, 12 MHz): `READY` 23
cycles after `lock_sync`, the release step is 8 cycles (not 4 as first
read), and it reruns after a lock drop and after a one-cycle glitch. With
`RST` = `!rstb` alone, `READY` never returns after the first drop.

Spec §6.4 updated (companion *Start-up logic*, new *Kuntur start-up*);
PLAN T2, K3.4 and the A.4 start-up item updated. Next: Manuel's RTL; a
sequencer testbench around `gddr_sync` (Claude); `cdr_tb` part 5 (DIVRST
at a new divider phase).

## Kuntur system start-up (new spec `system-startup.md` §1)

Manuel widened T2 to the start-up of the whole system, Kuntur first.
Traced from the MCU firmware, the FPGA RTL and pin file, and the board
(`kuntur144-ecl.kicad_pcb` + final BOM, as-built from Manuel):

- FPGA configures itself from SPI flash; `PROGRAMN`/`DONE` reach only J2.
- MCU: PA10 high at power-on starts the ROM bootloader (~5 s, recorded
  2026-08, unmeasured since), then MCO (32 MHz, the FPGA's `clkin`), then
  the 5 s debug delay.
- `rhd2164_controller` plays the RHD config table once, on the first clock
  edges, while the PLL is still locking; nothing waits for lock.
- **FPGA `rstb` (E3) floats**: R15 (to PA10) is not fitted and the `.pdc`
  has `PULLMODE=NONE`.
- As built: R12 fitted (MCO → `clkin`), R13 removed, U4 fitted but unused,
  R15 not fitted. All rails come up together.
- The real PA10 net (the 2026-08 search followed TP5 = PB1): MCU U1.L5 →
  R15 → FPGA E3, J2.4.
- LIFCL power-on reset (TN-02099 §5, §8): covers configuration and wake-up
  on the internal oscillator; flip-flops start at their initial values,
  nothing knows about `clkin` or the PLL. A user `GSR` primitive exists.

Decided (Manuel): reset = power-on reset (flip-flops initialised "in
reset") + PLL lock with a hold-off (~1 ms on `clkin`); lock loss resets all
of Kuntur's logic; regbank status word polled by the MCU instead of a fixed
delay; measure boot time without the debug delay (O-S1); debug delay
development-only. Written into `docs/interfaces/system-startup.md` §1;
`lvds-tunnel.md` §6.4 (Kuntur's `gddr_sync` takes `!rst_n_clkin`) and
PLAN updated. The companion (§2) is next.

## Companion system start-up (`system-startup.md` §2)

Traced from `intan_rec_adapter.v`, its `.pdc` and pad report, and the eval
board guide (EB-02028). Three clock domains come up independently:
supervisor (`clkin` 12 MHz, always), receiver (`wclk`, only with Kuntur
and the cable), emulator (Intan `sck`, only with RHX). Found:

- `rstb` (G14) is button SW2 with a board pull-up, but the pad has
  Radiant's default `PULLMODE:DOWN`; the two fight.
- One `rstb` resets all three domains; nothing else resets anything.
- The emulator's registers come from `initial` blocks, so it passes RHX
  detection with no Kuntur, cable or `clkin`: kept as a requirement.
- Until `DONE` MISO is high-impedance: RHX started first finds no chips.

Decided (Manuel): bitstream in flash for operation, JTAG in development; a
reset per domain rooted in the power-on reset; the emulator never reset by
link events; the link-down flag starts *down* (sentinel until the first
valid frame, a T5 requirement); status LEDs; JP2 a board requirement.
Written into §2 (R-C1–R-C7, order with RHX and Kuntur, timeline,
verification, O-S2). `lvds-tunnel.md` §6.4: the companion's `gddr_sync`
`RST` and `cdr_rstb` now take `rst_n_sup` instead of `rstb`. PLAN: new A.4
item, T2 row updated.
