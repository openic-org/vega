## impl_25k: first eye scan

`python3 mosi_eye_scan.py --port /dev/ttyACM1 --rate 25` (bridge on
`ttyACM1`; `ttyACM0` is the other ST-LINK), Kuntur power-cycled before.
672 points, dwell 10,000, 8,872 commands, 787 s (~11 commands/s). CSV:
`pc-app/bench/mosi_scan_25k_20261110_042415.csv`. Restored at the end
(`mosi_sel` 4, CHK_EN 0, PHASE_POS 0, table). Exit 1 (no Δ*).

- **chip0: errors at every point, both halves**, Δ −19.8 … +32.9 ns.
  The first-error answer is `0xFFFF` throughout: MISO undriven or stuck
  high, the "chip0 not responding" signature (CLAUDE.md, liveness). The
  few rows where FIRST_ERR is a chip1 half are WRITE(6, FF) answers:
  expected `FFFF`, which chip0's `FFFF` happens to match.
- **chip1: clean from −19.763 to +28.625 ns** (48.4 ns of a 52.7 ns
  bit, 2T at T = 26.35 ns). Fails from +28.70 ns on (`mosi_sel` 3), the
  predicted end of the map past +T (the word shifts by one bit: chip1 A
  answers `0000` / `00FF` for `FFFF`). No hold-side edge down to −3T/4.
  Today's launch (Δ = 0) has ≥ 19.8 ns before it and 28.6 ns after.
- Counts below FFFF beside errors at FFFF (e.g. 43,432 / 65,535) are
  the read order: CHK_COUNT is read first, the error words after, all
  saturated by then. Harmless for the verdict (it uses errors = 0).

**Reading:** on this boot chip0 does not answer at any MOSI phase,
including the 48 ns where chip1 is clean. So this failure is not MOSI
timing (PLAN.md: "an all-FFFF failure at every phase rules timing
out"). Caveat: chip0 is intermittent over hours (A.1.2); this is one
cold boot. It says nothing yet about chip0's eye when it does answer.
