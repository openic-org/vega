### S5a: the short NRST press

The companion re-sweeps on every LOCK drop it sees, and a re-sweep is a
visible ~2 s of LED2. So when the LEDs do not change, the companion's
LOCK did not drop. Three ways that can happen:

1. **Kuntur was not reset.** A short press stops MCO only briefly; if
   `pll0`'s LOCK never falls, Kuntur's generator is not cleared and
   `TUN_CLK` never stops for the hold-off. If the PLL output also stayed
   clean, the word boundary is unchanged and the link is still right:
   no recovery was needed.
2. **Kuntur was reset, but the companion kept LOCK.** `TUN_CLK` stopped
   for ≥ 1.024 ms and the companion's PLL did not drop LOCK. The
   companion stays on the old word boundary: wrong data, LED3 on. This
   is `cdr_tb`'s `MUT6=3`.
3. **Kuntur was not reset, but its clock glitched.** MCO stopped too
   briefly for LOCK to fall, yet long enough to disturb `pll0`'s output.
   A glitch on Kuntur's `ECLK` can move `ECLKDIV`'s divide phase and with
   it the transmit word boundary, without any reset at either end. Same
   symptom as 2.

The bench can separate case 2 from 1 and 3: a scope on `TUN_CLK` with a
timeout trigger (> 500 µs without an edge) shows whether Kuntur restarted.
Only a data check after lock separates 3 from 1.

**More from the bench (Manuel):** every NRST press stops `TUN_CLK` (seen
on the scope), so case 1 and case 3 are out for those presses. Some
sessions every press moved the LEDs, others not; the LED change came up
to **4–5 s after the press**; once the LEDs would not respond to NRST at
all until SW2 was pressed, after which the behaviour was good again.

**Reading (Claude), first try, wrong:** the 4–5 s was put down to the MCU
waiting in its ROM bootloader with MCO off. Manuel then measured that
`TUN_CLK` comes back as soon as NRST is released, so the stop lasts only
as long as the press (plus Kuntur's 1.024 ms hold-off), and the delay is
not explained.

**What stands:** `cdr` leaves `SUCCESS` only on a LOCK drop (no
data-based exit), and every companion timer is short (supervisor 16
cycles, `gddr_sync` 23, sweep 2 s). So any LED change comes from the
companion's `pll0` LOCK (non-sticky, `PLL_LOCK_STICKY(0)`) falling, and
a press with no LED change at all means LOCK never fell although
`TUN_CLK` stopped and Kuntur's word boundary moved: case 2, data wrong
under LED3. When LOCK falls relative to the stop and the return is the
open question. To measure: scope `TUN_CLK`, LED1 (F13, high = off) and
LED2 (G13), peak detect, ~10 s per screen, trigger on the press.

**Decision (Manuel):** no more probing of this now. Better detection on
the companion side (more RTL) comes after the animal test; for the test
the system is run so it does not arise. Spec: `system-startup.md` O-S3.
**Runbook rule for R4 (proposed):** bring Kuntur up first, then the
companion (the S3 order, which locked first time); after any Kuntur
restart (MCU reset) press the companion's
SW2 and wait for LED2 → LED3 before trusting the data. SW2 also resets
the emulator, so do it with RHX acquisition stopped.
Never plug or unplug the pigtail with either board powered: it carries
power (S6).

**Open for the runbook (Manuel):** (a) JTAG-only loading means the
companion must be reloaded after every power-up, or the flash programmed
before the test (then O-S2 matters); (b) with DC-coupled LVDS, is
powering one board *up* with the other unpowered and the cable in as
risky as powering one down? The proposed order (Kuntur first, then the
companion) and today's S3 both had one side powered alone. If so, the
runbook needs a safe sequence (e.g. both from one switched supply).

**Decided (Manuel):** (a) every bitstream goes into its board's flash
before the animal test (then measure O-S2, 12 V → DONE, from flash);
(b) power order stays as used so far: **Kuntur on first, then the
companion; companion off first, then Kuntur**, until other sequences can
be tested safely.

**Runbook items for R4, from T3:** the power order above; never plug or
unplug the pigtail with either board powered; after a Kuntur MCU reset
press SW2 (RHX stopped) and wait for LED2 → LED3 (O-S3); RHX shows the
sentinel for about 2 s after the companion starts (R-C4); JP2 installed
(R-C7); LED meanings (R-C6).
