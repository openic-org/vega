**Bench sequence per power cycle (Manuel):** power on → reprogram the
Kuntur FPGA (needed after every power cycle today) → the FPGA streams its

**`SET_CHANNELS` cannot reach the chips (RTL, read-only):** `ch_a`/`ch_b`
go from `regbank.v` only to `ch_sel.v`, which picks which received word
goes to the FIFO; `stream_enable` only gates `fifo_wen`
(`ch_sel.v:83`). The RHD2164 SPI sequence runs the same whatever the
channels are, so an apply changes no traffic on CS/SCLK/MOSI. Today's
"death after an apply" timing is therefore most likely coincidence (or
indirect, e.g. MCU activity on the shared supply), not a mechanism.
