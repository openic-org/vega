### Why G1's source changed, not only its size

The old text sourced `CONFIG` from "the config table walked out of
`RB_CONFIG_BASE`". Two things are wrong with that:

- `rhd2164_controller` walks the config table **once** after reset and
  never returns to it, so a later table write does not reach the chips.
- Writes injected through sampling entry 32 (MCU diagnostics, §10's
  ZCHECK marker on registers 5–7) change the chips without touching the
  table.

A shadow updated from every `WRITE(R, D)` the SPI master shifts out sees
both paths. It also needs no regbank read port (both are taken, K7). One
MOSI drives both chips, so one byte per register is the whole state.
R-K5 (config done) is now needed by the tunnel: no `CONFIG` before the
walk is finished.

## Rates for the animal test, and the tunnel's rate (decided, Manuel)

- **Production and the animal recording: 28 kSPS**, wireless (`clk`
  42.504 MHz). Unchanged.
- **Comparison against the Intan controller: 25 kSPS**, wired and
  wireless together (`clk` 37.95 MHz). The controller offers 25 or 30 only.
  **The tunnel targets 25 kSPS only**; wired at 28k is dropped as a goal.
- **33 vs 35 commands.** Kuntur keeps 33 (one alternate command, no slots
  wasted). RHX sends 35 (32 `CONVERT` + 3 aux). They need not match: the
  emulator decodes each command and answers the aux ones itself (§9.2,
  §9.1b), and only the frame rates must agree, which Kuntur's `clk` sets:
  37.95 MHz = 25,000 × 33 × 46. Manuel's first idea was dummy answers for
  the two extra commands plus clock adjustment in the companion; the dummy
  answers already exist, and the companion has no clock of its own on this
  path (receive side from `TUN_CLK`, Intan side from SCLK).
- **The comparison also runs in the animal: Kuntur reflashed mid-surgery,
  target 5–10 min.** PLAN.md R6 lists what has to be settled.
- No new `mode_id` for now.

## G5: lock in frame mode (decided, Manuel: option c)

The companion's sweep needs the K4.2 pattern, which is never sent with
frames, and Kuntur cannot know when the companion would need it. Options
were (a) a training window after each Kuntur reset, (b) sweep on the
frames' known bytes, (c) fixed phase. **Chosen: (c).**
- Two builds per end: bench (pattern + sweep) and operating (frames +
  fixed phase). K4.3 stays build-time.
- The stored phase comes from R1a at 37.95 MHz on the pigtail in use;
  margin rule ≥ 25% of a bit (`DIV + 1` steps) from any error, both sides.
- Frame-mode lock: two consecutive good-CRC `SAMPLE` frames with
  `frame_index` + 1. Loss: PLL lock lost, or 8 consecutive CRC failures
  (re-hunt at the same phase). `frames_crc_failed` watches the eye.
- Aux frames carry the `frame_index` of the `SAMPLE` they follow.
- Idle stays `0x00`.

`lvds-tunnel.md` §6.6 *Frame mode*, §4.7, §12 step 4; PLAN.md T4, R1a.
