## Summary

The A.1.1g chain closed end to end on the desk side. `kuntur_tb.sv` became a
**self-checking** testbench — 27 SystemVerilog immediate assertions, all passing

Two findings outweigh the checklist:

- **A latent failure that would have been misdiagnosed.** `FPGA_SPI_ReadSamples()`

## 1. Testbench: from waveform aid to self-checking artifact

`kuntur_tb.v` → `kuntur_tb.sv`, compiled as SystemVerilog (`qrun.f` updated).
Immediate assertions (`assert (c) $display(...); else begin ... $error(...) end`)
with a pass/fail summary and `$finish`, so a red run is visible without opening
a waveform.

Structural changes:

- **`spi_xfer(word)` task handshaking on `mcu_done`** instead of the
  `#12000/#300/#600` triple that appeared 15×. There is now no settle delay to
  re-tune when SPI timing changes. Gotcha worth remembering: it must be
  level-sensitive `wait()`, not `@(negedge)` — `mcu_done` falls a few `clk2`
  cycles after `mcu_start` rises, i.e. *inside* the start pulse, so an
  edge-triggered wait misses it and blocks forever.
- `reg_write16` / `reg_read16` / `fifo_pop_pair` wrappers.
- Command-encoding functions replacing all the `data_mosi_*` parameters, which
  also cleared both housekeeping items (dead `data_mosi_a`/`_b`, and the flat
  `8'h84` that hid its field structure).

Coverage: T1 reset defaults · T2 16-bit write · T3 read settling · T4 control
taps · T5 pairing under interleaved traffic · T6 write-tag aborts · T7 broken
POP pair. 27 checks, 0 failures, 2.25 ms sim against a 4 ms guard.

## 2. The pairing claim — confirmed, and confirmed *non-vacuously*

The result A.1.1g-tb existed to get.

T5 issues four POP pairs separated by a deliberately-odd 3-transfer write, a
`REG_READ`+`NOP`, and a lone `NOP`. ChA read `0001, 0002, 0003, 0004` with ChB
exactly +1000 every time.

The part that matters: **the counters advance.** Had the FIFO been empty the
pairs would have returned the `0x8000/0x8000` underrun sentinel, which
`check_pair` accepts — and every check would have passed proving nothing. An
advancing counter shows each pair consumed a fresh entry.

Retires three MCU-side workarounds: the even-batch rule, `FPGA_SPI_Init()`'s
priming transfer, and the NOP padding.

**Correction to a recorded assumption.** `PLAN.md` claimed the RHD model's
canned `0xe7xx`/`0x81xx` would make a swap "eyeball-obvious." They never reach
the FIFO — `ch_sel` computes `data0_synced`/`data1_synced` and discards them,
emitting `{cnt0, cnt0+1000}` (`components.v:423-425`, the A.1 ramp bug). Better
for this test, not worse: the invariant becomes numeric, `ChB - ChA == 1000`,
with a slip flipping it to −1000 (= 64536). **But it dies with A.1.1e** — see §6.

## 3. A broken POP pair fails loudly, as designed

T7's incidental result. ChA jumped `0004 → 0006`: the lone POP fired `fifo_ren`
at `op_decode1`, popped entry `0005`, delivered its ChA, then aborted when the
partner was not a POP — so entry 5's ChB was never clocked out. One entry lost,
bounded, no phase slip afterwards.

This is the closest thing yet to a direct test for the historical 32-bit FIFO
channel-swap bug, and it is what made §4 findable.

## 4. The latent failure in `FPGA_SPI_ReadSamples()`

Not on any task list; found while writing the MCU rewrite.

`FPGA_SPI_Init()` discarded one transfer, then every `ReadSamples()` call issued
`2n`. The running transfer count was therefore permanently **odd**, so pairs ran
`(T1,T2) … (T117,T118)` with `T119` dangling into whatever came next. Harmless
while nothing checked.

Under the A.1.1g FSM a POP whose partner is not a POP aborts **and consumes the
offending transfer**. `STOP_STREAMING` is a `REG_WRITE` issued right after a
`ReadSamples()` call, so its tag-1 transfer would have been swallowed; tags 2
and 3 would then each abort on their own tag check, and **streaming would never
stop.**

The reason this is worth a section: it would have surfaced as the pc-app's
"no confirmation received" timeout — indistinguishable from the USART-overrun
limitation already documented and accepted in `PLAN.md` A.2. Prime candidate for
being blamed on something already known to be flaky, and burning a bench session.

**Fix:** `ReadSamples()` is now self-contained — `2n+1` transfers with a trailing
`NOP` carrier, no priming, no dangling pair. Exactly the POP-POP-NOP shape T5
validated. Costs ~5 µs per BLE packet (1 transfer in 119 at 59 pairs).

`StreamFlushFpgaFifo()` was batched at 32 pairs/call as fallout: called one pair
at a time, `2n+1` means 3 transfers per pair instead of 2 — a 50% regression on
a blocking operation bounded at 16 384 pairs. Batching amortises the NOP to
~2.03/pair. Also fixed an off-by-one in its log line, which counted the
terminating sentinel pair as a discarded sample.

**Unverified:** this changes the 30 kSPS hot path, whose `spi_bb_transfer` NOP
margins were tuned empirically against SKP rates over several sessions. Those
margins are untouched, but the transfer count per packet is not. Needs bench
validation — `FPGA_SPI_DebugDumpPairs(32, 1000)` over the debug UART gives
DUP/SKP/OFF directly, and any `OFF` means the pairing offset is wrong.

## 5. MCU rewrite and spec revision

`fpga_spi.c`/`.h` rewritten against the new protocol:

| Function | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| `FPGA_SPI_Init` | 1 priming transfer | 0 |
| `FPGA_SPI_SetChannels` | 2, offsets 4/5 | 6, words 196/197 |
| `FPGA_SPI_ReadChannels` | 4 (incl. padding) | 3 |
| `FPGA_SPI_SetStreamEnable` | 2 | 3, word 228 |
| `FPGA_SPI_ReadStreamEnable` | 2 | 2 |
| `FPGA_SPI_ReadSamples` | `2n` + init priming | `2n+1`, self-contained |

Spec sections 1, 1a and 4.1 revised first, per working principle 5. Three
factual errors found and fixed while in there, all of which the RTL contradicts:

1. §1 said `addr` is *"ignored on `REG_READ`"*. It carries **tag 1**, with the
   target address in the data byte. Field renamed `addr` → **`tag`** — it never
   carried an address, and the mismatched name is what let the stale description
   survive.
2. §1 said a mismatched tag aborts to `op_nop3`; the RTL has only `op_nop0`
   (§1 said `op_nop0` correctly nine lines earlier).
3. §1's register table put `rhd2164_sampling_cmd0-3` at **128–131**. They are at
   `RB_CTRL_BASE + 0..3` = **192–195**. That row was also malformed — four cells
   in a three-column table. `stream_enable` word 164 → **228** in three more
   places across the spec and `PLAN.md`.

The spec is **reopened**, with a per-hop status table. The change is confined to
SPI0 — BLE 0xFFF1/0xFFF3, the bridge, and the pc-app are untouched. Sections
1/1a/4.1 are marked as *intended*, not as-built, since the MCU side is written
but unrun.

## 6. A.1.4 revisited — subsumed by A.1.1g

Prompted by asking what A.1.4 still means after the week's changes. Reading the
RTL rather than the plan:

```
cnt0 = 32  →  rb_addr1 = RB_SAMPLING_BASE + 32 = ram[80]
           →  regbank_dout1  →  rhd_dtx  →  spi1 MOSI
```

**The placeholder slot is already wired.** `rb_addr1` spans the full 0–32 range
and `ram[80]` is initialised to `RHD_READ(6'd63)`, labelled *"RHD2164 Sampling:
cmd0"* (`components.v:600`). The slot fetches and transmits that command every
frame, today. There is no consumer to wire.

**A.1.1g deleted the reason for `rhd2164_sampling_cmd0-3`.** They existed
because the sampling table was unreachable and writes were 8-bit; now `ram[80]`
is writable at full width via `reg_write16(80, cmd)`. The four registers are
dead-end wires — and `kuntur_fpga.v:76` declares only `cmd0-2`, so `cmd3` is not
even connected. They should be deleted before someone wires to them believing
they are the intended path. Frees words 192–195, and means A.3's impedance-check
DAC needs no reserved block either.

**So the ladder's gate moved to A.1.1e.** You can inject any command; you cannot
see the answer, because `ch_sel` still emits the ramp. Reading the placeholder's
result needs no new RTL either — the RHD answers two commands later, so slot
32's value lands at slot `(32+2) mod 33 = 1`, i.e. `ch_a = {2'b00, 6'd1}`. The
`ch_a` reset default already carries the comment *"Remember there is a delay of
2 SPI cycles"*.

**Do A.1.1e together with the A.1 structural fix.** Beyond the plan's own
"avoids touching `ch_sel` twice": A.1.1e kills T5's `ChB - ChA == 1000`
invariant, which exists only because of the ramp. A selectable test-pattern mode
keeps that assertion alive instead of silently voiding it — and is the same mux
`doctor` (B.6) and the B.5 pre-session self-test need to push a known pattern
through the real path on assembled hardware.

Flagged for A.1.1d: 33 slots with a fixed 2-slot delay puts slots 31 and 32's
answers at slots 0 and 1 of the *next* frame, across the `ch_is_0_redge`
boundary that latches `data0_synced`. The placeholder sits on the awkward side.

## 7. The design cannot be simulated outside Questa

This design does not simulate under **iverilog**: it compiles clean with
`-g2012`, then time stops advancing at **t = 60 ns**, right as `rhd_start` first
pulses. A bare probe with no testbench logic at all — no MCU master, no RHD
model, MISO tied low — reproduces it. Questa runs the same source correctly, so
it is an iverilog artifact rather than an RTL fault.

Leading suspect: `rhd2164_controller` (`components.v:879`), whose hand-written
sensitivity list `always@(current_state or rhd_done or cnt0_is_max)` drives
`rhd_dtx_sel` → `max` → `cnt0_is_max`, feeding back into its own list. It should
settle, since `rhd_dtx_sel` is a pure function of `current_state`, but
`always@(*)` removes the question and is the right fix regardless.

Recorded as a **B.1 item, argued as a blocker rather than a convenience** — the
existing B.1 line "wired into the CI checks so a red run blocks a merge" is
unimplementable on a licensed GUI simulator, and a contributor who cannot
simulate cannot safely change RTL. That is the same barrier as B.6's licensed
Lattice toolchain, which the plan already calls the worst OOBE blocker. Approach
recorded cheapest-first: audit the hand-written sensitivity lists, then evaluate
Verilator (`--timing`) as the CI simulator with Questa kept for waveform debug.
Required outcome: a `make sim` any contributor can run with no licensed
software.
